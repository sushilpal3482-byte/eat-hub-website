From Prayagraj Hotel to Eat Hub.

Our journey began over two decades ago on January 26, 2003, as Prayagraj Hotel. For years, we have been a staple in the Khadoli industrial area, serving honest, home-style North Indian meals to hardworking families and professionals.

Today, we are evolving into Eat Hub. While our name is updating, our core promise remains exactly the same: Ghar Jaisa Khana. By moving to direct WhatsApp ordering, we are eliminating third-party app commissions (like Swiggy and Zomato) entirely. This means you get the freshest food, delivered by our own trusted team, at the fairest possible price.
